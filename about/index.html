Quick Solution Vehicles handles general mechanical repairs and specialist engine rebuilds from its Sparkbrook workshop. Call with your registration and the work you need, and we’ll discuss the next step.

A clear repair decision before major work

Our approach starts with the complaint, the engine or vehicle specification and the evidence from inspection. A warning light does not automatically identify a failed component, and a non-runner does not automatically require another engine. We discuss assessment, findings and the scope before major work proceeds. Engine rebuilds typically take 1–3 weeks depending on parts availability and internal damage. The standard engine-rebuild warranty is 6 months or 10,000 miles, whichever comes first; exact cover confirmed in writing.
