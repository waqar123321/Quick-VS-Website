Workshop enquiries

Quick Solution Vehicles LTD, Company No. 16525397, uses information you provide by phone, email or WhatsApp to respond to your enquiry, assess the work and administer an agreed repair. This can include your name, contact details, vehicle registration and fault description. Relevant information may need to be shared with an agreed recovery provider, parts supplier or warranty provider where necessary for the work. Contact info@quickvs.com about how your enquiry information is handled or to request access, correction or deletion, subject to any applicable record-keeping requirements.

Online quote requests

The quote form collects your vehicle registration, name, phone number, job type and any mileage or message you choose to provide. We use these details to handle your enquiry and take steps at your request before a repair agreement. Web3Forms processes and forwards your submission to our workshop email. We also receive the page the enquiry came from. If you accept optional measurement cookies, a successful request records the job category and page path in Google Analytics. Your registration, name, phone, mileage and message are kept out of analytics.

Analytics and ad measurement cookies

If you accept, we use Google Analytics 4 and Google Ads conversion measurement to understand page use, contact-button clicks and which Google ads lead to enquiries. Google Analytics cookies (_ga and _ga_*) distinguish browsers and keep visits together. Google Ads measurement cookies (_gcl_au and _gcl_aw) help link ad clicks to enquiries. These cookies are only set after you consent; the Google tag is not loaded before you accept. We do not use them for personalised advertising. Contact-click events record the contact method and page, not your registration or message. Only Google ad click identifiers and UTM campaign labels are retained from page URL query parameters; other parameters and fragments are excluded. Google processes this measurement data. We rely on your consent for these cookies and the related personal data processing. Rejecting leaves every workshop contact option available. Your choice is stored in your browser for 180 days. You can withdraw or change consent at any time using Cookie settings in the footer; rejecting stops measurement and clears these cookies from this site.

External contact services and retention

Opening WhatsApp or Google Maps takes you to a service operated by that provider, whose own privacy terms apply. Your email provider and telephone service also process communications through their systems. We retain enquiry and repair information for as long as needed to handle the work and applicable business records. Avoid sending information that is unnecessary for your vehicle enquiry. You can contact the workshop at 56a Stratford Street North, Sparkbrook, Birmingham B11 1BP, or email info@quickvs.com.
