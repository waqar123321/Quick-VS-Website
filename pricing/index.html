Workshop FAQs

Before you book

Can I get an estimate before bringing my car in?

Tell us the registration, mileage and work needed. For straightforward known jobs we can discuss an estimate; faults and major repairs may need inspection before the scope can be confirmed.

Are engine rebuild starting prices fixed quotations?

No. Published starting prices are for the relevant engine family and are not a promise that every damaged engine can be rebuilt at that figure. The written quote states the work included after inspection.

Are parts and labour included?

The written estimate or quotation for your job will identify the agreed work and what is included. Do not assume an unrelated repair, additional damage or recovery is included unless stated.

Do you charge for MOT testing?

We are not an MOT test centre. We can assess and repair mechanical failures and advisories before the vehicle returns to a testing station for its MOT or retest.

Is vehicle recovery included?

Recovery for suitable non-running vehicles can be discussed and arranged at customer cost. Confirm the arrangements before transport.

Speak to the workshop

Need a service, repair or fault checked?

Send your registration, mileage and the work needed or symptoms. For an MOT failure, share the report; for a non-runner, call before arranging delivery.
