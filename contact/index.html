Workshop FAQs

Before you book

Do you offer free recovery or UK-wide recovery for engine rebuilds?

Yes. Recovery is FREE within 10 miles of our Sparkbrook, Birmingham (B11 1BP) workshop when you go ahead with an engine rebuild. For vehicles further across the West Midlands or anywhere in the UK, we arrange flatbed recovery with upfront pricing.

Do you charge for diagnostics if I book an engine rebuild?

Engine diagnostics are £50, and that £50 diagnostic fee is waived in full when you proceed with an engine rebuild with us.

Do Ingenium engines use wet timing belts?

No. JLR 2.0 AJ200D/AJ200P engines use timing chains. We assess timing chain, balance shaft, turbo and oil dilution faults.

Which engines do you assess for wet-belt concerns?

Wet-belt-equipped Ford 1.0 EcoBoost (from £850), Ford 2.0 EcoBlue Transit/Custom (from £1,050) and earlier 1.2 PureTech variants (from £650), usually completed within 48 hours of drop-off.
