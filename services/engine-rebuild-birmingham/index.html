Diagnosis before major work

Engines fail for many reasons: timing faults, lubrication problems, overheating, oil contamination, component wear or previous poor repairs. The correct route depends on the damage found during inspection.

Symptoms to report

What has changed?

  • Knocking or rattling
  • Smoke or overheating
  • Oil pressure warnings
  • Loss of compression
  • Non-start or seized engine

Describe when the symptom started, whether warning lights appeared and whether the engine still runs.

Workshop FAQs

Before you book

Is rebuilding cheaper than replacing an engine?

Sometimes. It depends on the damage, vehicle value and parts availability.

Do you rebuild petrol and diesel engines?

Yes, where rebuilding is commercially sensible.

Do you provide prices online?

Yes. Our pricing page lists starting prices for selected engines. A final quote is confirmed after inspection.

How long does an engine rebuild take?

Typical rebuild timeframe is 1-3 weeks depending on parts availability and damage extent.

What warranty is included?

Engine rebuilds include 6 months or 10,000 miles, whichever comes first; exact cover confirmed in writing.

Can you recover the vehicle?

Yes, recovery can be arranged at customer cost.

How much does an engine replacement cost?

A used engine supplied and fitted starts from £1,395 for common smaller engines. Send your registration for a quote on a larger diesel. A used replacement carries a warranty of 3 months or 3,000 miles, whichever comes first.
