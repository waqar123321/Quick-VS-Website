Diagnosis before major work

Engines fail for many reasons: timing faults, lubrication problems, overheating, oil contamination, component wear or previous poor repairs. The correct route depends on the damage found during inspection.

Planning a local workshop rebuild in Birmingham

Our workshop is at 56a Stratford Street North, Sparkbrook, Birmingham B11 1BP. This page covers the practical booking and assessment route for Birmingham drivers, while our rebuild-process page explains the work itself. Start with the registration, engine details and symptoms, then tell us whether the vehicle still runs. If it has stopped elsewhere, call before arranging delivery. We need to understand the vehicle's condition and access requirements so the first assessment can be planned without repeated transport or unnecessary running of a damaged engine.

From first enquiry to collection

The initial conversation helps identify the right next step; it cannot confirm unseen internal damage. We discuss diagnostic work, strip-down when appropriate, the parts route and the quotation before major work proceeds. Typical turnaround is 1–3 weeks depending on parts availability and internal damage. Keep any previous diagnostic report or service history available, especially if the engine has already been replaced. At collection we explain the agreed work and relevant handover care, and accept debit cards, contactless/mobile NFC and bank transfer.

Diagnosis and timing repair options

When the extent of damage is unclear, start with engine diagnostics and mechanical fault finding before agreeing strip-down work. For chain-driven engines with viable repair options, read about timing chain assessment and replacement. A timing repair and a full rebuild require different scopes; the fitted engine and inspection findings determine the route.

Symptoms to report

What has changed?

  • Knocking or rattling
  • Smoke or overheating
  • Oil pressure warnings
  • Loss of compression
  • Non-start or seized engine

Describe when the symptom started, whether warning lights appeared and whether the engine still runs.
