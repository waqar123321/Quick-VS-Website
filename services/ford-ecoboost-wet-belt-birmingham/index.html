Which Ford 1.0 EcoBoost engines use a wet timing belt

Earlier Ford 1.0 EcoBoost petrol engines fitted to the Fiesta, Focus, EcoSport, B-Max, C-Max and Mondeo use a wet timing belt running inside the engine oil. Later 1.0 EcoBoost versions switched to a timing chain for the camshaft drive while retaining a wet oil-pump belt in many applications, so the badge alone is never enough to quote accurately.

Send your registration, mileage and service history so we can verify the exact timing layout fitted to your car. If you also run a commercial van, see our dedicated Ford Transit 2.0 EcoBlue wet belt page. For all wet-belt makes together, see our Birmingham wet belt hub.

Why the oil pickup strainer check matters on EcoBoost engines

Because the belt runs submerged in oil, ageing or wrong-spec oil causes the rubber belt to shed fibres and teeth. That debris collects in the sump and can restrict the oil pump pickup strainer, starving the turbocharger and bearings of oil pressure.

A planned wet belt replacement includes the timing belt, applicable oil-pump drive belt, tensioners, required seals and fixings, plus correct-specification oil and a new filter. Where belt deterioration or debris is found, checking the oil pickup and lubrication system is critical before returning the car to service.

Warning signs: stop immediately if the red oil light appears

Common early warning signs include a flickering or solid red oil-pressure light, brake-vacuum stiffness, turbo whine, metallic rattling or engine management faults relating to cam timing. Many EcoBoost belts degrade silently until the pickup blocks.

If the red oil-pressure warning comes on, stop safely and switch the engine off immediately. Do not keep driving or restarting it. Call us before moving the car: if an engine rebuild is required, we offer free recovery within 10 miles of our Sparkbrook B11 workshop and waive the £50 diagnostic fee when you go ahead with the rebuild. For fault checks before committing, see engine diagnostics.

Before you book

How much is a Ford 1.0 EcoBoost wet belt replacement in Birmingham?

Planned replacement on applicable 1.0 EcoBoost engines starts from £850. Final price depends on the exact engine specification and condition and is confirmed in writing before work begins.

How long does an EcoBoost wet belt change take?

Usually within 48 hours of drop-off for a planned replacement. If there is existing belt debris, low oil pressure or internal damage, extra inspection time is discussed first.

Does my Ford Fiesta or Focus have a wet belt or a timing chain?

It depends on the engine version and build year. Later 1.0 EcoBoost engines changed to a chain camshaft drive. Send your registration and mileage so we can confirm your exact setup before quoting.

What if my EcoBoost engine has already lost oil pressure or seized?

Stop driving immediately and call us. A new belt alone cannot reverse bearing or internal damage. See our all-makes engine rebuilds page — we offer free recovery within 10 miles and waive the £50 diagnostic fee when you proceed with a rebuild.

What warranty and payment terms apply?

Wet belt replacements include a 6-month or 10,000-mile warranty (whichever comes first, confirmed in writing). We take a parts deposit upfront, with the balance due on collection by debit card or bank transfer.
