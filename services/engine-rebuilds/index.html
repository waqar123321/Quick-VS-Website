What a complete engine rebuild involves

An engine rebuild is a measured repair of the existing engine after strip-down, rather than simply fitting a different used unit. The work required depends on what is reusable. A cylinder head repair, replacement timing kit and a complete bottom-end rebuild are different jobs, and the quotation should reflect that difference. We assess the crankshaft, bearings, pistons, cylinder bores and head before agreeing the final scope. Parts and any machining are selected around the engine code and the damage found, rather than an assumed standard list.

Find the failure cause before rebuilding

Knocking, low compression, overheating and an oil-pressure warning can point to different failure routes. The first question is why the engine stopped working. Oil starvation may involve the pump, pickup or oil feed; overheating may have started with a coolant leak; a timing failure may have damaged valves and pistons. Where metal has circulated through the oil, related components also need assessment. Replacing damaged internals without investigating the original cause can put the repaired engine at risk when it is started again.

Repair, rebuild or replace: agreeing the scope

A rebuild is worth considering when the main castings and repairable components provide a sound basis. Severe cracks, excessive bore damage or parts availability can change that decision. We explain the findings and discuss rebuilding against replacement before major work proceeds. The quote should identify the parts, labour, machining and associated repairs that are included. Tell us about earlier repairs and any previous engine replacement, as the fitted unit may differ from the original registration record. Workshop FAQs

Before you book

Is a rebuild the same as a used engine replacement?

No. A rebuild repairs and measures the existing engine. A replacement fits another engine; its condition, history and specification must be checked separately.

Can you quote from a noise recording?

A recording helps with the first conversation, but internal condition cannot be confirmed remotely. Final scope and price depend on inspection.

Will the original cause be checked?

Yes. Timing, cooling, lubrication and related faults must be considered alongside the damaged internal components.
