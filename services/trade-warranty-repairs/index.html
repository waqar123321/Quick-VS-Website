Evidence for a major mechanical authorisation

Trade and warranty-company enquiries need a clear record of the complaint, vehicle details and any work already carried out. Send the registration, engine details, mileage, symptoms and existing diagnostic reports. For a claim, confirm the provider's authorisation process before a strip-down begins. Inspection and dismantling may be chargeable even if a claim is declined, so responsibility for those costs needs agreement. A fault-code list alone may not explain internal engine damage; photographs, measurements and the repair findings can be relevant to the next decision.

Scope, parts and communication during repair

An engine claim can involve more than the visibly damaged bearings, pistons or head. Lubrication, cooling, timing and contaminated associated components may affect the repair. We discuss the findings and the proposed scope before major parts are ordered. The approved parts route, machining requirements and labour must be clear, especially when a provider imposes limits. We keep the repair conversation focused on confirmed damage and practical options. Additional findings can change the scope, and should be agreed rather than assumed to fall within an earlier estimate.

Recovery and workshop planning

Suitable engine and major mechanical work can be considered from outside Birmingham where recovery is arranged or funded by the customer. Contact us before sending a non-runner, with its location, access details and whether it rolls or steers. Collection arrangements, diagnosis, repair and any provider approval are separate stages. Typical engine-rebuild turnaround is 1–3 weeks depending on parts availability and internal damage, with updates when the repair route is clearer. Tell us about deadlines and vehicle-use needs early so the booking can be discussed around the actual work.

Workshop FAQs

Before you book

Does a warranty company automatically cover diagnosis?

No. Coverage and authorisation depend on the policy and provider. Agree responsibility before diagnosis or dismantling.

Can a trade customer send an engine non-runner?

Contact us first with the fault, location and recovery details so suitability and access can be discussed.

Can additional damage change the quote?

Yes. Strip-down can reveal further damage. The revised scope is discussed before additional work is agreed.
