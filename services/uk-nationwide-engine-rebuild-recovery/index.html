We reply within 30 minutes in opening hours with an upfront flatbed recovery quote (if your address is within 10 miles of 56a Stratford Street North, Sparkbrook, Birmingham B11 1BP and you go ahead with an engine rebuild, local recovery is free — outside 10 miles, recovery is always quoted upfront before anything moves).

3. Once you confirm collection, your car or van is transported directly to our Birmingham workshop, inspected on arrival, and quoted in writing before rebuild work starts.

What access and non-runner details to share before dispatch

To avoid wasted call-outs or unsuitable trucks, tell us three things before we book collection: whether the handbrake/electronic parking brake releases, whether an automatic gearbox can select Neutral (important for locked SUVs and 4x4s), and whether the pickup spot has narrow gates, underground clearance limits or steep driveways.

Do not keep cranking a knocking or seized engine to move it onto a kerb — winching a non-runner onto a flatbed safely prevents turning a repairable engine into scrap metal. See our engine rebuild assessment checklist for what history to send.

Why drivers ship vehicles to our central Birmingham hub

Being right in the middle of the UK motorway network (M5, M6, M42, M40) keeps flatbed transit distances shorter from London, the South West, Manchester, Yorkshire, Wales and the East Midlands.

Our 4-ramp workshop handles BMW N47, BMW N57, JLR 2.0 Ingenium, JLR 3.0 TDV6/SDV6, Ford Transit 2.0 EcoBlue, performance and SVR V8 engines, and used engine supply and fitting (from £1,395, 3 months / 3,000 miles warranty) if strip-down shows a block is beyond economical repair.

Diagnostic credit, handover and written protection

Our £50 Snap-on diagnostic fee is credited in full against any repair booked on the same visit. Rebuilds typically take 1–3 weeks depending on machining and parts availability and carry a 6-month or 10,000-mile written warranty (whichever comes first).

You can collect your repaired vehicle directly from Sparkbrook (10 minutes from Birmingham New Street / Moor Street rail stations) or arrange return transport quoted upfront. Pay by debit card or bank transfer.

Workshop FAQs

Before you book

Is UK-wide vehicle recovery free?

No — recovery from outside 10 miles of our Sparkbrook, Birmingham (B11 1BP) workshop is quoted upfront before collection. Addresses within 10 miles of B11 1BP qualify for free recovery when proceeding with an engine rebuild.

Can you collect from London, Manchester, Bristol, Wales or Scotland?

Yes. Send your pickup postcode, registration and whether the vehicle rolls freely so we can give you an upfront flatbed collection quote.

What if my car is currently sitting at another garage or main dealer?

We regularly collect non-runners directly from third-party garages and dealerships once you have settled any storage/inspection balance with them and authorised release.

How do I get my car back once the rebuild is complete?

Most nationwide customers take the train into Birmingham New Street or Moor Street (10 minutes from our workshop) to drive home, or we can quote return flatbed delivery upfront.

What happens if strip-down shows the engine cannot be rebuilt?

We explain the exact damage before you commit further spend and quote our used engine supply and fitting option where suitable.
