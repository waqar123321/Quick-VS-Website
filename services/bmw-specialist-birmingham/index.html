Servicing and everyday BMW repairs

For BMW servicing, send your registration, mileage and any service records. We agree the maintenance needed for the fitted engine. Brake repairs, suspension and steering work and clutch enquiries are quoted by registration.

Tell us whether you need planned work or have a new noise, vibration or warning light. Additional work is discussed before proceeding.

Warning lights, poor running or a timing rattle

A rattle does not prove that the timing chain needs replacing. Describe when it happens, any warning lights and whether the engine still runs. Engine diagnostics helps establish which checks are needed before parts are chosen.

Our timing chain service covers chain concerns separately from confirmed internal engine damage. If there is an oil-pressure warning or heavy knocking, stop safely, switch off and call before arranging delivery. Do not keep restarting the engine to record the noise.

When your BMW needs engine work

An identified BMW N47 engine fault or BMW N57 repair enquiry has its own assessment route. We also consider B47 rebuild enquiries. The damage and reusable components determine whether rebuilding is sensible.

For a performance model, use our performance engine enquiry page and send the engine details. A starting price does not confirm all parts or fitting inclusions; the final scope is agreed after inspection.

Workshop FAQs

Before you book

Do you work on petrol and diesel BMWs?

Yes. We handle BMW petrol and diesel enquiries for general mechanical work and engine assessment. Send your registration so the fitted engine and job can be identified.

Can you look at a timing chain rattle?

Yes. The noise needs assessment before a chain replacement is assumed. Tell us when it happens and share any existing fault report. Do not repeatedly start a distressed engine to demonstrate it.

Do I need to bring the car in for a quote?

Send your registration, mileage and the work needed first. We can discuss the assessment route, but a fault or internal damage may need inspection before the final written quote. Call before arranging delivery of a non-runner.

Do you only carry out BMW engine rebuilds?

No. We handle servicing, diagnostics, brakes, suspension, clutches and other mechanical repairs as well as engine work. Quick Solution Vehicles is an independent all-makes workshop.

Is the online starting price the final price?

No. The engine, findings and agreed parts and labour determine the final price. Additional work is discussed first and the price is agreed in writing before work.
