Workshop FAQs

Before you book

Do you work on Jaguar as well as Land Rover?

Yes. We handle Land Rover and Jaguar petrol and diesel enquiries as part of our independent all-makes workshop. Send the registration and symptoms so the fitted engine and work can be identified.

Can you investigate an Ingenium timing chain rattle?

Yes. A rattle needs assessment before parts are chosen. Tell us when it happens and send any previous fault report. The 2.0 Ingenium is chain-driven.

Do all Range Rovers have the same engine?

No. Different models and versions use different engine families. The registration and engine code, if known, help establish the right assessment and parts.

Can you quote before I arrange delivery?

Send the registration, mileage, running status and symptoms first. We can discuss the assessment route; a final price may require inspection. Call before arranging transport.

Is there a starting price for SVR or supercharged V8 work?

No. Those jobs are quote only. The fitted engine, damage and agreed repair scope determine the quotation.
