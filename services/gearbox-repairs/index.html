Separate gearbox faults from related components

Difficulty selecting a gear, vibration under load or a driveline noise can originate outside the gearbox. A clutch that does not fully release, worn mounts, a linkage fault or driveshaft wear can produce similar complaints. The transmission type and when the symptom occurs guide the first checks. Tell us whether it happens stationary, while moving, during gear changes or only when hot. We investigate the mechanical cause before suggesting an expensive replacement, and explain when the evidence points towards a specialist internal transmission assessment.

Leaks, noises and mechanical inspection

Visible oil leakage needs identification because engine oil, gearbox oil and other fluids can collect in similar places. We look at seals, accessible housings, mounting points and associated parts, then consider whether fluid level or contamination is relevant to the reported fault. A road test can help distinguish a wheel-speed-related noise from an engine-speed-related noise where the car is safe to drive. Internal gearbox repair suitability depends on the particular unit, findings and parts route. We agree that scope rather than advertise every gearbox as the same fixed repair.

Agree the route before removal

Removing a gearbox can provide access to clutch, flywheel and rear-engine-seal areas, but those parts should only be included when the findings justify them. We discuss diagnosis, removal and any additional assessment separately, particularly if the vehicle has had previous transmission work. Existing reports and details of earlier fluid changes are useful. If a gear cannot be selected or the driveline is locking, arrange advice before attempting further driving. The aim is a clear repair decision that accounts for the complete driveline, the vehicle's condition and the cost of the agreed work.

Workshop FAQs

Before you book

Does a gear-selection problem prove the gearbox is damaged?

No. Clutch release, hydraulics, cables, mounts and other associated parts can cause selection problems.

Do you rebuild every automatic gearbox?

The work we can undertake depends on the specific transmission and diagnosis. Call with the vehicle details before booking.

Can clutch and gearbox checks be combined?

Yes. Their symptoms often overlap, so both systems may need consideration before the repair scope is agreed.
