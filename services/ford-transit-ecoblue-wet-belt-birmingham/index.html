Every day a Transit or Transit Custom sits off the road costs you work. Planned 2.0 EcoBlue wet belt jobs are scheduled cleanly at our 4-ramp Sparkbrook workshop and are usually completed within 48 hours of drop-off.

Send your registration, mileage and service history so we can verify parts before you bring the van in. If your belt has already snapped or caused internal damage, see our Ford Transit 2.0 EcoBlue engine rebuild page.

What is included in a planned 2.0 EcoBlue wet belt job

On applicable 2.0 EcoBlue engines, the replacement covers the timing belt, applicable oil-pump drive belt, tensioners, seals and required single-use fixings, plus correct-specification oil and a new oil filter.

Because degraded rubber material can contaminate the sump and oil pickup strainer, checking lubrication condition is part of doing the job properly rather than simply swapping a belt. Any additional wear or fault found during strip-down is agreed in writing before proceeding.

When to replace a Transit 2.0 EcoBlue wet belt

Ford revised replacement schedules for EcoBlue wet belts downward over time, and heavy urban stop-start use, high idling hours and fuel dilution from DPF regenerations accelerate belt wear.

Do not wait for a rattling noise or low oil-pressure warning. If your Transit has an unknown belt history, high mileage or approaching interval age, booking a planned £1,050 replacement prevents a far more expensive breakdown. For general van servicing, clutches or brakes, see servicing and clutch replacement.

Already broken down? Recovery and transparent terms

If your Transit EcoBlue has lost drive, snapped its belt or triggered a red oil warning, do not keep cranking the engine. For engine rebuilds, we offer free recovery within 10 miles of Sparkbrook B11, arrange UK-wide recovery with clear upfront pricing, and waive the £50 diagnostic fee when you go ahead with a rebuild.

Wet belt replacements and rebuilds carry our 6-month or 10,000-mile warranty (whichever comes first, confirmed in writing). Parts deposit is taken upfront and the balance is settled on collection by debit card or bank transfer.
