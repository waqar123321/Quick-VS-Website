## Before campaign launch
Create the relevant conversion actions and obtain the actual Ads ID and labels. The site can then use one Google tag for GA4 and Ads, with consent controls retained.

Actual website calls using a Google forwarding number need a separate call conversion action and phone snippet. This is different from a dial-link click.

Current GA4 events:
- phone_call_click
- whatsapp_click
- email_click
- directions_click

A WhatsApp click does not prove a conversation or booking. A telephone click does not prove a connected call. Treat clicks as intent signals and plan primary conversion goals around qualified calls, genuine enquiries or confirmed jobs. Avoid importing the same event and tracking it directly as two primary conversions.

For an existing action, open its details, then Tag setup > Install the tag yourself to obtain its event or phone snippet. Do not insert unconfigured tags into production.
