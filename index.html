Workshop FAQs

Before you book

Can you repair a car that has failed its MOT?

Yes. We repair mechanical MOT failures and advisories, including brakes and suspension. We do not conduct MOT tests or retests; those are carried out at a testing station.

Do you replace control arms and wishbones?

Yes. We inspect suspension and steering faults, including control arms, wishbones, bushes and ball joints, then agree the repair needed. We do not provide wheel alignment or tracking.

Can I book ordinary servicing or brake repairs?

Yes. We handle oil and filter services, brake discs and pads, clutches, diagnostics and other general mechanical work as well as engine rebuilds.

Do you rebuild engines across all makes?

Yes. We handle petrol, diesel, performance and commercial van engine rebuild enquiries across all makes and engine ranges.

What warranty is included?

Our standard engine-rebuild warranty is 6 months or 10,000 miles, whichever comes first; exact cover confirmed in writing.

Sparkbrook workshop, wider Birmingham customers
