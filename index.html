Workshop FAQs

Before you book

Can you repair a car that has failed its MOT?

Yes. We repair mechanical MOT failures and advisories, including brakes and suspension, with same-day turnaround available on routine repairs. We do not conduct MOT tests or retests; those are carried out at a testing station.

Do you replace control arms and wishbones?

Yes. We inspect suspension and steering faults, including control arms, wishbones, bushes and ball joints, then agree the repair needed. We do not provide wheel alignment or tracking.

Can I book ordinary servicing or brake repairs?

Yes. We handle oil and filter services, brake discs and pads, clutches, diagnostics and other general mechanical work as well as engine rebuilds, with same-day turnaround available on routine jobs.

Do you offer free recovery or UK-wide recovery for engine rebuilds?

Yes. Recovery is FREE within 10 miles of our Sparkbrook, Birmingham (B11 1BP) workshop when you go ahead with an engine rebuild. For vehicles further across the West Midlands or anywhere in the UK, we arrange flatbed recovery with upfront pricing.

Do you charge for diagnostics if I book an engine rebuild?

Engine diagnostics are £50, and that £50 diagnostic fee is waived in full when you proceed with an engine rebuild with us.

Do Ingenium engines use wet timing belts?

No. JLR 2.0 AJ200D/AJ200P engines use timing chains. We assess timing chain, balance shaft, turbo and oil dilution faults.

Which engines do you assess for wet-belt concerns?

Wet-belt-equipped Ford 1.0 EcoBoost (from £850), Ford 2.0 EcoBlue Transit/Custom (from £1,050) and earlier 1.2 PureTech variants (from £650), usually completed within 48 hours of drop-off.

What warranty and payment terms apply?

Standard warranty on engine rebuilds, wet belts, timing chains, cambelts and clutches is 6 months or 10,000 miles, whichever comes first; exact cover confirmed in writing. Parts deposit upfront; balance due on collection by debit card or bank transfer.
