Workshop FAQs

Before you book

Do you rebuild engines across all makes?

Yes. We handle petrol, diesel, performance and commercial van engine rebuild enquiries across all makes and engine ranges.

Do Ingenium engines use wet timing belts?

No. JLR 2.0 AJ200D/AJ200P engines use timing chains. We assess timing chain, balance shaft, turbo and oil dilution faults.

Which engines do you assess for wet-belt concerns?

Wet-belt-equipped Ford 1.0 EcoBoost, Ford 2.0 EcoBlue and earlier 1.2 PureTech variants. Other versions, including Ford 1.5 EcoBoost, are identified before the timing design is specified.

How long does an engine rebuild take?

Typically 1–3 weeks depending on parts availability and internal damage.

What warranty is included?

Engine rebuilds include 3 months or 10,000 miles on parts and labour.
